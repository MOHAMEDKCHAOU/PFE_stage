# Faymoos monetization model

Faymoos should sell **outcomes**, not page-builder features: a professional presence, interactive conversion experiences, Smart 360 spaces, and measurable leads.

## Recommended offers

| Plan | Indicative price | Best for | Included value |
|---|---:|---|---|
| Free | €0 | trial / students | 1–2 presences, limited guided experiences, basic analytics, Faymoos branding |
| Pro | €19/month | freelancers, creators, consultants | custom branding, richer analytics, 500 AI actions/month, 2 Smart Scan processing credits/month |
| Studio | €69/month | agencies / photographers / real-estate / designers | client workspaces, 12 Smart Scan credits/month, reporting, priority processing |
| Studio+ | €179/month | higher-volume agencies | 60 clients, 50 Smart Scan credits/month, higher AI/export limits, priority queue |

Prices are a product strategy starting point, not market research. Validate willingness-to-pay with interviews and checkout experiments before locking them.

## Extra revenue

1. Smart Scan credit packs, e.g. €9–€15 per extra processed space depending on compute cost.
2. Custom domains / white-label as Pro/Studio retention features.
3. Team seats for studios.
4. Premium templates for verticals such as real estate, architecture, art galleries and hospitality.
5. Lead integrations (CRM/webhooks) in paid tiers.
6. Later, optional booking/payment marketplace fee only if Faymoos actually processes transactions.

## Why people pay

A portfolio alone is hard to monetize. Faymoos combines: public proof → guided intent → CTA → lead → analytics → optimization. Smart 360 makes the offer particularly strong for real estate, interior design, showrooms, galleries, hotels and event venues.

## First commercial wedge

Do not launch to “everyone with an identity.” Start with a vertical where 360 and leads have obvious monetary value. Recommended first wedge: **real-estate agencies + interior designers + architecture studios**. Sell a complete interactive property/project presence, not software knobs.

## Sales motion

- Free self-serve creator acquisition.
- Pro upgrade when users want branding removal, custom domain, richer analytics or Smart Scan.
- Studio outbound to agencies with client management and bulk scan credits.
- Offer done-for-you onboarding for the first 20–50 customers to learn which workflows truly matter.
